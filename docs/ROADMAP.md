# Delivery roadmap

1. **Foundation:** private records, revisions, sharing snapshots, aggregate suppression and schema.
2. **Private journal:** accounts, encryption, capture, tags, search, calendar and personal patterns.
3. **Trusted sharing:** circles, membership, snapshot previews, reactions and comments.
4. **Public atlas:** pseudonyms, redaction, discovery, safe aggregates and moderation.
5. **Data rights:** exports, deletion, reminders, accessibility, deployment and complete browser coverage.
